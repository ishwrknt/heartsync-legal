Privacy policy

This policy explains how flexiorder handles information when you use HeartSync and this support website.

Updated 20 November 2026

Who operates HeartSync

HeartSync is operated by flexiorder. For privacy questions or requests, email flexiorderofficial@gmail.com.

HeartSync is a shared routine and personal connection app for two people. Using a cloud account lets you connect with a partner and save your records across devices. Optional features are described below; you can use the core app without choosing a mood, uploading a photo or sharing activity.

Information we handle

Account and preferences
Email address and authentication information, account identifiers, display name, time zone, daily schedules, reminder settings and quiet hours.
Routines and progress
Routines, checked habits, exercise or reading details you enter, timer and session records, completion history, shared commitments, recovery requests and reasons, streaks and earned achievements.
Partner communication
Private messages, shared thoughts, partner requests, heart interactions, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps and notification delivery records. Avoid putting unnecessary sensitive or medical details in messages or request reasons.
Optional photos
A profile photo you choose and photos proposed for your couple background. A shared photo requires the other partner’s approval. The app uses your device’s image picker; it does not need camera access to select a photo.
Optional mood and activity
A mood you explicitly select for your current shared day, and your latest activity check-in when you enable activity sharing. Some mood suggestions, such as period day or cramps, may reveal sensitive health-related information if you choose to share them.
Device and operational information
A Firebase notification token linked to your account and shared space when partner alerts are enabled, delivery status, security and correction audit records, and information needed to authenticate and protect the service. Local preferences and drafts are also stored on your device.

HeartSync does not request precise location, your contact list, microphone, wearable data or Health Connect data. It does not include advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, connect the correct partners, save and display routines and progress, evaluate shared commitments, deliver partner communication and optional notifications, support account and privacy requests, and protect records from unauthorized access or changes.

HeartSync does not sell your personal information. We do not use partner messages, photos, moods or routine records for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

Your current partner: information presented in your active shared space is available to you and your authorized partner. This includes the profile and shared photos, shared communication, routine/progress information and optional mood or activity that you choose to share. A partner’s own data is separate from your account.

Supabase: provides cloud authentication, database storage and realtime services. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: processes notification tokens and delivery data for enabled Android partner alerts. Android local reminders are scheduled on your device.

GitHub Pages: hosts this public support website. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs, to operate and secure its hosting. This website has no account sign-in, analytics script or tracking form. Following an email link opens your email application; information you choose to send is processed by the email services involved.

Providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy information is available at Supabase Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your optional sharing controls

Daily mood: a selection is shown to your current partner until midnight in your shared time zone. HeartSync does not infer a gender, menstrual cycle or diagnosis from it. There is no mood history or mood push alert. Expired choices are hidden, and the stored current selection is removed on a later current-space read or write; unlinking or deletion clears affected selections.

Activity sharing: starts off. If enabled, it shows approximate current/recent app activity based on the latest check-in, without a historical activity log or location. Offline devices can take a short time to reflect changes.

Photos: profile photos are optional. The app resizes selected photos and re-encodes them to remove original image metadata before upload. A proposed shared photo is visible to both partners for review and becomes the shared background only after the other partner approves that version. Either partner can remove the shared image.

Notifications and floating heart: you control notification permissions, sound and vibration. The optional Android floating heart requires separate permission to display over other apps and uses a visible service notification with controls. Its enable choice and screen position are saved on the device. Delivery and effects depend on the device’s settings and connectivity.

Storage and security

Cloud connections use HTTPS, authenticated access and database access controls. Android login credentials and the bounded recent offline journey copy use AndroidKeyStore-backed encryption. The offline journey copy excludes private message bodies, photos and unbounded archives, and is restricted to the matching account.

Device drafts, timers, private nicknames and ordinary preferences use app or browser storage. They are not an encrypted medical vault. Keep downloaded exports private and avoid shared browser profiles. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Account and routine information is kept while your account or shared space remains active. Your own saved workout archives are kept until you delete your account. You can remove supported optional content through the app and request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Account deletion removes your authentication identity, profile and associated personal records, including owned routines, personal sessions, messages, profile photo, registered notification devices and your private archives. It also ends your current shared membership. Your partner retains their own independent records and shared earned achievements.

Remaining ended-space operational records and shared content follow the service’s retention process. If records must be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations, we explain the affected categories and reason when handling your request. Backup copies are not instantly rewritten by a live account deletion; contact us for the retention details that apply to your account. We do not promise a fixed backup-erasure deadline on this page.

Short-lived heart gestures and mood displays can expire before their underlying records are physically removed. Expiry is not a promise of immediate erasure from all storage. Exported files and copies saved outside HeartSync remain under the control of whoever saved them.

See account deletion for the in-app option and a request method that works without reinstalling the app. Support confirms any identity verification steps, the expected timeframe and completion.

Access, corrections and other choices

In Settings you can manage reminders and effects, export currently authorized data, leave a shared space and delete your account. Leaving a couple creates an archive of your own workout history and earned shared records; it does not provide access to your former partner’s private logs or messages.

For access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, email flexiorderofficial@gmail.com. The options available may depend on your location and the information involved. We may verify that a request concerns your own account. Never send a password or one-time sign-in code.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud account. Removing device/app data removes local copies; it does not replace a cloud account-deletion request.

Changes and questions

We update this page when our handling of information changes. The date above identifies the latest published version. For questions, contact flexiorderofficial@gmail.com.
