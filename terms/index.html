Terms of use

Please use HeartSync with care for yourself, your partner and your information.

Updated 20 November 2026 · Operated by flexiorder

Using HeartSync

HeartSync helps two people track self-directed routines, share progress and communicate privately. These terms apply to your use of HeartSync and this support website. For questions about them, contact flexiorderofficial@gmail.com.

Keep your account information accurate and your sign-in credentials private. Only use accounts and shared spaces you are authorized to access. Choose carefully whom you connect with: the current shared space makes relevant information available to both partners.

Respectful participation

You control the routines and optional information you enter. Partner requests and approvals are a way to communicate; they do not remove anyone’s right to make their own choices or replace consent to something else. Do not use the app to threaten, coerce, harass or impersonate another person.

Only upload or share content you have the right to use. Do not attempt to bypass access controls, interfere with the service or access another person’s account or private records without authorization. You remain responsible for the messages, photos and other content you choose to share.

Routines and wellbeing

HeartSync provides habit and self-directed fitness tracking. It is not a medical device, diagnosis service, treatment plan or emergency service. Choose manageable activities, use rest when needed and seek a qualified professional for medical concerns. A shared streak or a partner’s approval is not a medical recommendation.

Availability and notifications

App features can change as HeartSync improves. Connectivity, Android permissions, battery settings, notification settings and service availability can affect synchronization and partner alerts. Do not rely on an app notification or live heart interaction for urgent or safety-critical communication.

Keep a copy of information you need independently of the service. Settings offers an export of currently authorized app data.

Your account and data

The privacy policy explains what we handle, partner access and your controls. Signing out, leaving a shared space, deleting an account and uninstalling are different actions. To request permanent account deletion, follow the account-deletion instructions.

Your partner keeps their own independent records when you leave or delete your account. Do not assume that leaving a space removes copies that another person already saved outside HeartSync.

Changes and contact

Changes to these terms are published here with an updated date. These terms do not limit rights you have under applicable law. If you have a concern about HeartSync, write to flexiorderofficial@gmail.com.
