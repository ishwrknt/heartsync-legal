ACCOUNT & DATA

Delete your account.

Request permanent deletion of your HeartSync account, even without the app installed.

HeartSync · Operated by flexiorder · Updated 20 November 2026

Request deletion by email

Send your request from the email address registered to your HeartSync account to flexiorderofficial@gmail.com.

Use the subject HeartSync account deletion and say that you want your account and associated personal data permanently deleted.

Email an account-deletion request

The button opens an email draft. Review and send it in your email app; opening this page or the draft does not submit a request. If no email app opens, use the address and subject shown above.

What happens next

  1. Send your request. Include your account email and a clear request for permanent deletion. No app reinstallation is required.
  2. Verify your identity if needed. We confirm that the request relates to your account and explain any additional verification. Never send your password, a one-time code or your partner’s sign-in details.
  3. Receive confirmation. Support confirms the expected completion timeframe after verification and notifies you when deletion is complete. If any information must be retained, we explain what and why.

Deleting your account is permanent. If you want a copy of your data, use Settings → Privacy & your data → Export my data before deletion, or ask support about access to your information.

If you still have the app

Sign in to the account you want to delete. Open Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete account, review the details and confirm. This is separate from signing out, leaving a couple or uninstalling.

If you cannot sign in, the email request above remains available. Your partner’s approval is not needed to request deletion of your own account.

Data included in deletion

  • Your authentication account, display profile and profile photo.
  • Your owned routines, personal commitments, workout/session records and private archives.
  • Your messages, shared thoughts, mood selection, registered notification devices and associated personal records.
  • Your active couple membership and access to the shared space.

Account deletion does not delete your partner’s separate account, independent records or shared earned achievements. Shared/ended-space content and any necessary retained records follow the privacy policy’s retention and deletion section.

Retained records and local copies

Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations; support explains any applicable retention when handling your request. Backup copies are not instantly rewritten when live account data is deleted. Contact support for the retention details that apply to your account.

Files you exported or copies saved outside the service are under their holder’s control. You can remove remaining device-local drafts or preferences by clearing HeartSync’s app/site data. Uninstalling by itself does not submit a cloud deletion request.

Want to remove specific data instead?

You can clear supported optional content in the app, such as a shared thought, photo or current mood. For another data-deletion request that keeps your account, email flexiorderofficial@gmail.com and describe what you want removed. We explain whether the requested removal affects app functionality.
